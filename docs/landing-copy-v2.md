**The three tools (left column, evidence) — KEEP:**

- **Discord** is ephemeral and overwhelming. A discussion about Chapter 3 disappears in a flood of memes.

**The turn (right column, resolution) — KEEP your wording:**

## 4. How it works — KEEP (one elevation on step 3)

- **Heading:** How GatherBound works

- **02 Read & Review:** Everyone reads on the same timeline. Letter-grade or star-rating reviews, emoji reactions, threaded discussions. Members can participate from anywhere.
